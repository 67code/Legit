Welcome to Legit

Your trusted watchdog against online scams and shady sellers.

What is Legit?

Legit is a community-driven platform that helps users verify the authenticity of online stores, especially dropshipping sites. We analyze customer reviews and seller history to flag potential scams.

Why use Legit?

  • Stay safe from malware-infested or scam websites
  • Read real customer reviews before making a purchase
  • Help the community by sharing your own experiences

🚀 Our Mission

We're building a safer online shopping world — one review at a time. Join our community and help others avoid scams, frauds, and unreliable sellers.
